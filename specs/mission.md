# Mission

Weather Quotes is a personal creative tool.

It takes the weather outside your window — right now, in your city — and finds the literary moment that matches it. A foggy morning becomes a passage from Dickens. A snowstorm becomes a page from Tolstoy. A warm still evening becomes Hardy.

The value is the moment of recognition: the world outside suddenly has a voice borrowed from literature.

## What it is not

- Not a productivity tool or a utility.
- Not a social platform.
- Not a search engine for literature.

## Success criterion

You open it, type your city, and feel something.
